Use it at your desk or in a workflow

Use the Windows app to choose sources, review a plan, and inspect the results. For recurring work, run the same engine from a script and read its structured progress and results.

Original files stay in place. You choose which outputs to provide to an AI tool.

The preview handles UTF-8 text, Markdown, CSV, and TSV directly, with 64 MiB source and bank text limits. Word and PDF extraction can depend on installed converters and may carry coverage holds.
